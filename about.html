About

Who I Am

I've been working in IT support for over 20 years. Started in the early days, worked through every generation of email systems, watched cloud computing change everything, and learned that good support is about solving actual problems for actual people.

I built Sentius from 75+ five-star client reviews on project work — proof that real businesses trusted me to get things done. But I realized I was doing one-off fixes when what most organizations actually needed was ongoing support and security management. So I transitioned to managed services to do that better.

I started Sentius because I got tired of the standard MSP model: overpromise, underdeliver, hide behind a sales team. I wanted to build something different.

Why Sentius Exists

The small problem is usually masking a bigger one

Most organizations I work with contact me with a small problem — an email migration, a configuration fix, updating an email signature. Once I look at their security posture, they realize the small problem was masking bigger ones.

A company with 10 years of Microsoft 365 might have ex-employee accounts still active with full access. Another might have zero MFA on any account, including admin. Another might have security alerts flagged for months that nobody even knew to look for.

Not because they're careless. Because nobody ever showed them what to look for.

I built Sentius to fix that. To be the person who knows what to look for, shows organizations where they actually stand, and helps them build security that works.
