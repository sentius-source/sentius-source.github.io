What We Do

Everything your Microsoft 365 needs to actually be secure

1

Microsoft 365 Setup & Migration

Whether you're new to Microsoft 365 or migrating from another platform, I handle the technical work: mailbox migration, DNS and MX record verification, device configuration, user training.

What this means: You move without disruption. Your data gets there safely. Your staff can access what they need, properly configured, from day one.

2

Security Assessment & Hardening

I take a look at your Microsoft 365 environment — your devices, your user accounts, your email security, your admin access. Most businesses have gaps they don't know about. I find them and help you fix them.

What this means: You'll know where the real risks are. You'll understand what needs to change. You'll see your secure score improve — usually significantly.

3

Email & Data Protection

Backup, recovery, email security, threat detection. If something goes wrong — or before it does — this is the layer that protects you.

4

Ongoing Management & Support

Once everything's set up, it stays working. Device patching, security monitoring, user support, licensing reviews, email security — whatever you need to keep things running.

What this means: You can focus on your business. I focus on keeping your systems secure and reliable.

5

Cyber Essentials Readiness

If you handle sensitive data or need to prove your security posture to clients, regulators, or insurers, you need Cyber Essentials certification. I help you get there. This isn't just ticking boxes — it's actually building secure systems and proving they work.

6

Licensing Reviews & Optimisation

Most businesses overpay for Microsoft 365 or have the wrong license tiers. I review what you actually need versus what you're paying for.

What this means: Often, a better licensing strategy saves you money while giving you better security.
