Common Questions

How this works

What's not included?

The pricing covers the service tiers listed above. One-off projects (migrations, complex configurations, training, Cyber Essentials readiness) are quoted separately.

Can I mix tiers?

Yes. Different user groups can be on different tiers. A finance department might need Complete tier while admin staff are on Protected.

What about extra support?

Each tier includes a certain amount of support per user per month. Beyond that, support is billed at £75/hour in 15-minute increments. No surprises.

Are there setup or onboarding fees?

Implementation and setup are quoted separately based on scope. Your initial free security review includes an estimate of setup costs.

Can I change tiers or cancel?

Yes. Monthly billing. Change tiers or cancel anytime with 30 days notice. No lock-in contracts.
