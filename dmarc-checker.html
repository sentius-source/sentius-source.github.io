What is DMARC?

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance) is an email authentication protocol that helps protect your domain from email spoofing and phishing attacks. It works alongside SPF and DKIM to ensure only authorized mail servers can send emails on behalf of your domain.

Prevent Spoofing

Stop attackers from sending emails that appear to come from your domain, protecting your reputation and customers.

Improve Deliverability

Proper DMARC configuration ensures your legitimate emails reach inboxes instead of spam folders.

Compliance Ready

DMARC is a requirement for many compliance standards including Cyber Essentials and industry best practices.
